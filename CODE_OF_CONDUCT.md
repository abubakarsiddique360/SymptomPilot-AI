# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participating in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Because this project touches health topics, we also commit to discussing medical
questions without shaming anyone for their symptoms, their knowledge level, or
the care they have or have not received.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assume good faith; ask a clarifying question before assuming a mistake.
- Respect privacy: never post another person's health information.
- Keep discussions about the code, not about the person writing it.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory comments of any kind.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information, including health data, without
  explicit permission.
- Dismissing someone's symptoms or discouraging them from seeking care.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, reviews or issue threads.

will explain the reasoning to the people involved where it is safe to do so.

## Medical disclaimer

This project does not provide medical care. Nothing in these spaces replaces
assessment by a qualified clinician, and no contributor should present
themselves as providing diagnosis or treatment here.
