Safety first

This assistant is educational. It cannot diagnose or rule out illness and never replaces a clinician. For chest pain, breathing difficulty, stroke signs or thoughts of self-harm, contact emergency services now.

Describe what you are feeling, how long it has been going on, and anything that makes it better or worse. I will tell you how urgent it looks and show the evidence I used — but I am not a doctor.
